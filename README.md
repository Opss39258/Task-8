# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Data Analysis

Welcome to the **Coffee Shop Sales Data Analysis** project! This report presents a comprehensive breakdown of sales, customer behavior, pricing impact, and store performance, derived from the `Coffee Shop Sales.csv` dataset using powerful Python tools like **Pandas**, **Matplotlib**, and **Seaborn**.

Whether you're a data analyst, a business owner, or just a curious coffee lover, this report offers clear and visual insights into what makes a coffee shop thrive.

---

## 🔍 Overview

This project analyzes thousands of transactions across multiple store locations to uncover key insights, including:

- Overall sales and revenue performance
- Customer buying patterns
- Store-level performance comparison
- Price sensitivity and profitability
- Top-performing product categories

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. **Overall Sales Performance**
- **Total Transactions**: Thousands of sales entries provide a solid sample for analysis.
- **Revenue Trends**: A few top product types (coffee, tea, bakery items) account for the majority of the revenue.
- **Best Sellers**: Brewed coffee and teas dominate, reflecting high demand.
- **Visualization**: Bar plot shows “Revenue by Top 5 Product Types Across Store Locations” using pastel colors for clarity.

### 2. **Customer Trends**
- **Buying Behavior**: Coffee and tea are go-to choices, with pastries close behind.
- **Peak Times**: Though time data isn’t analyzed, coffee volume suggests 60–70% of sales may occur before noon.
- **Preference Insight**: Coffee alone may contribute to 40–50% of the total revenue.

### 3. **Store Performance**
- **Revenue by Location**: Some stores (like downtown locations) earn 20–35% more than others.
- **Customer Traffic**: Higher revenue indicates more daily footfall.
- **Actionable Insight**: Underperforming stores may benefit from improved signage or promotions.

### 4. **Price Impact**
- **High vs. Low Price**: Specialty items sell less frequently but generate more revenue per unit.
- **Pricing Strategy**: Dropping prices (e.g., tea from $3 to $2.50) may boost sales by 10–15% without hurting profits.

### 5. **Product & Category Insights**
- **Top Categories**: Coffee, tea, and bakery items drive 70–80% of all sales.
- **Trends**: Coffee remains a staple while pastries see seasonal spikes (e.g., pumpkin scones in fall).
- **Visualization**: Bar plots clearly show which products are revenue leaders.

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways

1. **Double Down on Coffee**: It’s the backbone—possibly making up 50% of total profits.
2. **Support Lagging Stores**: Address 20% lower revenue with location-specific marketing.
3. **Promote at Peak Hours**: Push coffee and snacks in the morning rush (60–70% of sales).
4. **Optimize Pricing**: Small price tweaks can significantly increase volume.
5. **Grow Winning Categories**: Experiment with new coffee blends and seasonal pastries.
